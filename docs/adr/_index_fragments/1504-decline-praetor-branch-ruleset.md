| [ADR-1504](1504-decline-praetor-branch-ruleset.md) | Decline praetor's branch ruleset template. The template (two approvals, code-owner review, signed commits) was never the live declaration, and praetor's renderer cannot express one approval without code-owner review, so it cannot be edited to match. `.standards.yaml` declines `branch-ruleset`, `.github/rulesets/main.json` is removed and `repository-security-policy.json`, checked against live, is the single declaration. Replaces the "kept as a local declaration" statements of ADR-1249 and ADR-1351. | Accepted | security, ci, governance, praetor, fork-local |
